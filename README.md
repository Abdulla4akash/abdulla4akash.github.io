# Intelligent Systems for Health

Public website for **Intelligent Systems for Health (ISH)**, a nonprofit implementation-science and health-systems research group based in Bangladesh.

ISH studies how health technologies can be adapted, implemented, evaluated, and sustained in urban and rural health and public-service systems. Research priorities include workforce capacity, staffing and training, technology adoption, workflow integration, equitable access, infrastructure readiness, resource constraints, operational feasibility, costs, and sustainability. Cost-effectiveness and effects on services and health outcomes are questions for evaluation where evidence permits.

**S M Abdulla Al Mamun** is the Principal Investigator and a Commonwealth Scholar. His technical background and previous AI/ML research are documented separately from ISH's institutional mission.
