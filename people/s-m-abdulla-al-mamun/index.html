Research

S M Abdulla Al Mamun is the Principal Investigator of Intelligent Systems for Health (ISH), a nonprofit research lab based in Bangladesh, and a Commonwealth Scholar with an artificial intelligence background at the University of Manchester.

His technical work spans machine learning, medical-image research, simulation, and research software in Python. He is interested in how AI changes the ways people communicate, learn, and cooperate, and in building research tools that make those questions testable.

Research and software

Medical imaging and interpretable AI

Research on transformer-based classification of malaria from blood-cell images, comparing vision transformers, Swin transformers, and convolutional models. The work combines medical-image processing, model evaluation, and visual explanations using Grad-CAM.

TrafficTwin: intelligent transportation and edge computing

An auditable research platform for dynamic resource management in vehicular edge computing. TrafficTwin combines SUMO traffic simulation with experiments on task offloading, scheduling, admission, and placement across roadside infrastructure. The research examines reinforcement learning and deterministic policies using reproducible accounting of latency, deadlines, and rejected tasks.

TrafficTwin on GitHub.

Livestock and environmental health

Vision-transformer research on transboundary disease detection and waste management in Bangladesh's livestock farming systems, using multi-task and transfer learning.

PaperStack: research software and literature workflows

A literature research platform for querying and reasoning over research papers. The work combines document ingestion, hybrid semantic and keyword retrieval, reranking, and graph-based exploration of research relationships.

MasalaEngine: retrieval-augmented generation

An academic project exploring retrieval-augmented generation over a culinary corpus. It combines dense retrieval, BM25, cross-encoder reranking, and comparative evaluation of chunking strategies and grounded responses.

MasalaEngine on GitHub.

SkillSight UK: skills analysis with NLP

A research pipeline for extracting a 13-category skills taxonomy from UK data and business analyst job postings. The project studies lexical baselines and evaluation methods using an annotated corpus, nested cross-validation, and bootstrap analysis.

SkillSight UK on GitHub.

Biomedical and bioinformatics studies

Research projects include reference augmentation for fish DNA barcoding in Bangladesh, thalassemia and HbE referral from complete blood counts, and selective review of qPCR amplification curves.
