Role at ISH

Tanveer Mahmud is a Policy & Partnerships Associate at Intelligent Systems for Health (ISH), focusing on the institutional conditions required for promising interventions to move from research into sustainable real-world use.

His contribution concerns stakeholder mapping, the requirements for partnership development, and pathways from evidence to implementation across public, nonprofit, academic, and service-delivery settings. This work examines organisational sustainability and the conditions needed for responsible scale-up.
