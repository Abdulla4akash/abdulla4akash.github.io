Intelligent Systems for Health

A nonprofit research lab based in Bangladesh, exploring artificial intelligence, health, and the ways people work with intelligent systems.

Principal Investigator

S M Abdulla Al Mamun leads ISH. A Commonwealth Scholar with an artificial intelligence background at the University of Manchester, his work spans medical-image research, simulation, and reproducible research software.

Meet the Principal Investigator →

Research focus

Medical-image analysis and interpretable machine learning; simulation and decision-support tools; and how AI shapes communication, cooperation, and learning.

Published research

The PI coauthored An Interpretable Transformer-Based Approach to Classify Malaria from Blood Cell Images, published in the IEEE i-COSTE 2024 proceedings.
