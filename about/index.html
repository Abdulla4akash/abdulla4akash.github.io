About Intelligent Systems for Health

ISH explores artificial intelligence, health, and human–AI interaction under the leadership of Principal Investigator S M Abdulla Al Mamun.

Intelligent Systems for Health (ISH) is a research lab led by S M Abdulla Al Mamun, Principal Investigator and Commonwealth Scholar.

The lab's research interests span medical-image analysis, interpretable machine learning, simulation, reproducible research software, and the ways AI shapes communication, cooperation, and learning.
